# Community courses

Links 2003 players have made well over a thousand free courses with the
game's course designer. The boot screen suggests a few of the most popular
ones and links to their pages on [Links Corner](https://linkscorner.org/),
the volunteer-run site that hosts most of them. The list is in
`game/scripts/course/community_courses.gd`.

This project doesn't host the courses. Players download them from Links
Corner and add them to the game:

- **Browser:** after loading your own game files, press **Add a course...**
  and pick the course's `.zip` download (or its `.crz`). The course files are
  unpacked in the browser and kept there for your next visit.
- **Desktop:** put the `.crz` (and any `.sha`/`.tip` next to it) in the
  `Courses` folder of your game folder.

Course files are locked with the same key as the disc courses, which the
course loader reads from the player's own `LINKSM_1.EXE`, so they play only
with your own copy of Links 2003. Stock ground textures a course refers to
come from your game's `STDTER_1.LIB`.
